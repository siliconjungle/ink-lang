`scan(xs).zip(scan(ys), combine)` into `scan(xs.zip(ys, combine))`.
The result length is exactly min(length(xs),length(ys)), including empty and
unequal independent inputs. No index-width or equal-length premise is needed.
The two generalized initial accumulators are combined using the same operation.

states; an inner second-list induction exposes the paired heads. Twelve support
objects transport those functional induction hypotheses; six public endpoints
are optimization roots. These are standard modular prefix identities, not six
independent mathematical discoveries. F32 is excluded because rounded prefix
arithmetic is not a homomorphism.
