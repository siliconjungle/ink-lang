1. `xs.filter(p).concat(ys.filter(p)) = xs.concat(ys).filter(p)`, for two independent lists and the same arbitrary pure total predicate. Order is xs then ys, even when either is empty or their lengths differ. Predicate evaluations are unchanged. The target has two stages rather than three, but copies rejected elements into the concatenation.
