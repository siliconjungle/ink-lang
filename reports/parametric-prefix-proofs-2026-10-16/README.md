## Construction cost and unchanged serving code

Six paired full producer invocations alternate previous/new order. The recorded
128-operation proposal plus normal checks takes about 2.6/1.1 seconds (2.41×
paired geometric improvement). Every sample is retained. The corpus evaluation
ran concurrently, so this is a workstation diagnostic rather than an exclusive
machine measurement or a general compiler speed claim.

The 32/128-operation selected core and emitted JavaScript are byte-identical to
the earlier sharing study. The audit checks those identities. Consequently its
[warm serving results](../shared-helper-code-2026-10-16/README.md) remain the
relevant serving evidence; this proof change makes no additional warm speed
claim. Expanded layout still emits its prior selected code, but its proof
packaging changes. The historical 512-operation failure remains valid at the
older study's source and is not overwritten.

## Frozen planning corpus

All ten frozen programs compile and admit rewrite alternatives. There are 54
admitted laws, seven reused across multiple programs. No alternative is selected:
all ten reach the measurement-budget gate. Nine stop at the time budget; median
planning time is 93.35 seconds. Near misses include 2,231 premise rejections,

attribution, context, inputs, plans and receipts are retained in `corpus.tar.gz`.

of a held-out speedup from the new prefix proofs.
