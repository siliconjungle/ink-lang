Investigate whether growing Ink's knowledge database makes real software faster
without changing the compiler. Work solo; do not run a swarm.

Record exact compiler, backend, runtime, ABI, planner and knowledge revisions.
Freeze execution machinery within each experimental epoch. Grow database knowledge
and authoring data; put necessary compiler changes in a separate epoch. Read
docs/composable-knowledge.md and docs/proof-growth-epoch3.md before making claims.

Research actual libraries and systems for reusable algorithms: sequence processing,
parser/AST traversal, editor text structures, drawing/document models, particle
simulations, spatial queries, indexes, incremental views, bulk state updates and
compact collections. Use upstream source and primary research. Work within current
types and semantics; arbitrary JSON, CRDTs and new host capabilities are out of scope.

Prioritise intermediate elimination, ordered fusion, repeated traversal removal,
duplicate computation elimination, unused-work removal, early-exit/search
alternatives, maintained summaries/indexes and unchanged structure reuse. Compose
existing families before duplicating their laws or helper bodies. Supply derived
implementations through typed database-authored algorithms and their imports.

Do not target an arbitrary proof count. Distinguish new reusable families from
type instances, support lemmas, adapters and duplicate endpoints. Explicitly label
stress fixtures; never count them as new optimisation families. Conditions must
be proved at every application site. Measurements, hashes, producer identity and
database membership establish no mathematical truth.

Freeze an independently selected evaluation corpus before developing its
optimisations. Separate development programs from held-out source programs, and
development inputs from held-out distributions. Use realistic upstream-inspired
workloads and actual upstream implementations where equivalent comparisons are
possible. Label partial kernels as partial kernels, not full applications.

At cumulative database checkpoints, use the SAME compiler, programs, inputs and
measurement boundaries. Track applicable coverage, alternatives discovered,
admitted and automatically selected, complete execution cost, measured memory,
discovery/checking/compilation/setup cost, regressions and uncertainty. Preserve
all raw samples and pins.

Separate forced-alternative capability measurements from ordinary automatic
selection. Keep the original baseline and strongest existing optimisation chain.
A shorter proof or larger catalogue does not imply a faster program. Never quietly
change weighting or remove adverse cells between checkpoints.

Use interleaved repeated measurements, consume complete outputs, verify input
immutability and include empty, small, large, early/late/absent-match and adversarial
cases. Preserve wrapping arithmetic, floating-point behaviour, reduction order,
errors, events and snapshots. Charge conversions, capture, allocation and transfers
at the stated application boundary. Include construction, update, checkpoint and
restore costs for maintained representations; do not hide index maintenance costs.

Plot runtime, memory and compilation cost against reusable-family count and useful
coverage alongside entry count. Attribute gains to families and combinations with
removal/ablation experiments. Report overlapping contributions; do not add their
speedups together. Explain confidence intervals and the limits of the corpus.

When an alternative is blocked, identify whether expression, discovery, proof
admission, lowering or profitability selection is the limiting stage. Do not conceal
the gap with a source-specific shortcut or an unrecorded compiler change.

Publish checked database entries, reproducible tooling, source provenance, raw
evidence and charts. Follow repository validation, independent-package boundaries
and exact pinning. Coordinate integration with concurrent compiler work, preserving
frozen historical epochs. The goal is convincing evidence of reusable accumulated
knowledge improving independently chosen software, not a large catalogue or an
attractive chart.
